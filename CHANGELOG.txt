Dying Light - Randomiser Mod - Changelog
========================================

2.0
---
New randomisers
    - Randomise enemy AI (Experimental): enemies keep their body but get another enemy's behaviour
      (same body shape only), e.g. a Goon acting like a biter, a viral acting like a Suicider,
      a Rais soldier fighting like Tahir.
    - UI colours: Accents or Everything, with an optional "Icons too" for HUD, radar and map icons.
    - World colours: Tinted or Wild sun, sky, cloud, water and star colours.
    - Dialogue: Same character or Everyone, in Harran and The Following.
    - Shuffle subtitles: subtitles of spoken lines swap with each other, in Harran and The Following.
    - Quest rewards: side quest and encounter rewards swap for others of the same kind; cash varies.

New enemies (70 in the pool, up from 49)
    - Hellraid: skeletons (3 kinds), fire / lightning / poison adepts, Dark Knight and Minotaur, in Harran.
      Optional and experimental in The Following too.
    - The Mother, The Following's final boss, in both forms (viral and Volatile), in The Following and,
      optionally, in Harran.
    - The Following's human ghost, optionally in Harran.
    - The big military viral, optionally in Harran with its real look.
    - Friendly NPCs: armed and unarmed survivors, Ahmet, Babar, Faruk, a Tower soldier, a bodyguard
      and Jade. They fight zombies and won't attack you.

Improvements
    - Freaks of Nature in Harran now get their real Following behaviour, toughness and size.
    - Menu redesign: the Enemies section is just the main switches, and every enemy, group and DLC
      option lives in the "Enemy spawn pool selection" window.
    - New groups start switched off, including for people updating from 1.0.

Fixes
    - "Remove mod" now restores the game's original files byte for byte. Before, the game could still
      report a modified install after removing the mod.
    - The Following no longer crashes when the spawn pool holds only enemies it can't use.

Known issues
    - With Hellraid enemies or the "in Harran" options on, the Prison map and the Be the Zombie
      tutorial crash. The menu warns about this.
    - Street crowds of special infected can glitch or crash (unchanged from 1.0).
    - With enemy AI on, biters given a Goon's AI carry the hammer but still hit with their hands.

1.0
---
    - First release: randomised enemy spawns, street crowds, loot, shops, Quartermaster stock, weapon
      tiers and loot quantity in Harran and The Following, with a mod menu, seeds and an optional new
      roll every time the game starts.
